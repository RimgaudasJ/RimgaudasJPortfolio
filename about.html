About Me

Software Engineering Student | Embedded Systems & Game Developer

I am a Software Engineering student with a strong passion for building reliable software, interactive applications, and low-level systems. Alongside my academic studies, I actively pursue personal projects that allow me to expand my knowledge across game development, web technologies, and Linux-based embedded systems.

I recently completed a two-month internship at Teltonika IoT Academy, where I gained practical experience working with Linux environments, networking, embedded development, and systems programming. During the internship, I worked with Raspberry Pi hardware and networking devices, explored OpenWrt, deepened my understanding of the Linux kernel and networking stack, and strengthened my C programming skills. I also built a custom Linux system for the Raspberry Pi 3B using Buildroot with a cross-compilation toolchain created using crosstool-NG, and contributed a patch fixing an IP address inheritance issue in the netifd network management service.

Outside of embedded development, I enjoy creating games and experimenting with modern software technologies. In my free time, I continue to build personal projects that improve both my technical knowledge and software engineering practices.

I am currently seeking opportunities in the IT sector. If you are looking for a motivated and adaptable developer — or know someone who is — I would be glad to connect and discuss how I can contribute.
